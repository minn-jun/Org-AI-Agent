***Abstract***
**— This paper discusses the system for visual assistance assistant non-face-to-face activity assistance services to help blind and blind people with visual information during their daily lives. We would like to provide a limited number of helpers to help more people through non-face-to-face services. In addition, we would like to minimize the mutual anxiety between the blind and the helper in offline face-to-face activities due to pandemics such as COVID-19.
