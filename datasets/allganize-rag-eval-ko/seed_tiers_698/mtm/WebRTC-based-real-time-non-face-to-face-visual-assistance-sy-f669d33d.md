Abstract -  This  paper  discusses the system for visual assistance assistant non-face-to-face activity assistance services to help blind and blind people with visual information during their daily lives. We would like to provide a limited number of helpers to help more people through non-face-to-face services. In  addition,  we  would  like  to  minimize  the  mutual  anxiety between the blind and the helper in offline face-to-face activities due to pandemics such as COVID-19. The proposed system uses raspberry  pie-based  optician  aids  and  WebRTC  streaming technology to help visually impaired people check videos taken through  aids  in  real  time  through  streaming  and  translate videos.
